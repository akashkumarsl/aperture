# APERTURE — Narration Script

Voice-over script for the full scroll experience and the promo film. Timings
are **scroll-relative** (the site is scrubbed, so the visitor controls pace);
the seconds in brackets are the recommended pace for the linear promo cut.

**Voice direction:** calm, low, deliberate — a quiet authority (think Jony Ive
product film meets a research lab). Never salesy. Let silence breathe. All lines
land on a beat; never rush a fade.

---

## SCENE 1 — Origin (Raw Data) · `#intro`

> Black. A single line resolves out of the dark.

**[0:00]** *(whispered, almost to self)*
"AI has transformed models."

**[0:04]**
"But data engineering is still manual."

**[0:08]** *(a question, rising)*
"What if data could improve itself?"

> Camera drifts downward. An underground reservoir of black crude appears.
> Label resolves: **RAW DATA**.

**[0:13]** *(grounding, matter-of-fact)*
"Every AI model begins with raw data."

**[0:17]**
"Raw data has value. But raw data alone is not enough."

---

## SCENE 2 — The Refinery (Aperture) · `#refinery`

> Crude flows up through glass pipes into a refinery. The wordmark blooms:
> **APERTURE — The AI Data Intelligence Layer.**

**[0:22]**
"This is Aperture. The intelligence layer between your data and your models."

> Telemetry floods in — statistics, loss curves, confusion matrices, production
> failures, weather and lighting distributions.

**[0:28]**
"It observes everything. Datasets. Training runs. Production feedback."

**[0:34]** *(the key idea — slow down)*
"But Aperture does not rush to generate data."

**[0:38]**
"First, it understands. It builds a knowledge graph of the problem — and reasons
about what the model actually needs."

---

## SCENE 3 — Diagnose (Reasoning) · `#diagnose`

> Text: **"Diagnose before acting."** Nine intervention cards fan out.

**[0:44]**
"Diagnose before acting."

**[0:48]**
"The reasoning engine weighs every possible intervention against the real
bottleneck."

> Cards illuminate by confidence: Synthetic Data 87%, Domain Adaptation 72%,
> Active Learning 64%, Physics Simulation 58%. The rest stay dark.

**[0:53]**
"Synthetic data — eighty-seven percent. Domain adaptation — seventy-two."

**[0:58]** *(the discipline)*
"Collecting more data? Tuning hyperparameters? Not today. Intelligence is knowing
what *not* to do."

---

## SCENE 4 — Orchestration (Engines) · `#engines`

> Robotic arms wake. Five specialised engines activate. Crude black turns to
> luminous gold. Label: **OPTIMISED TRAINING DATASET.**

**[1:04]**
"Then Aperture orchestrates."

**[1:08]**
"Specialised engines execute in concert — synthesis, domain adaptation, physics,
balancing, annotation."

**[1:14]** *(transformation lands)*
"Impurities fall away. Crude becomes gold. A dataset shaped to exactly what the
model was missing."

---

## SCENE 5 — Results (Training) · `#training`

> Gold data pours into the training engine. Dashboards climb. The confusion
> matrix cleans to a bright diagonal.

**[1:20]**
"The optimised dataset flows into training."

**[1:25]**
"And every curve bends the right way. mAP up. Recall up. Robustness up. Loss and
failure rate falling."

**[1:31]** *(the thesis, quietly triumphant)*
"Not from more compute — from data that finally matches what the model needs."

---

## SCENE 6 — The Loop (Feedback) · `#feedback`

> Production: cars, factories, robots, cameras. Real-world failures return and
> flow back into Aperture. The knowledge graph grows.

**[1:37]**
"Then the model meets the real world."

**[1:41]**
"And the real world pushes back. Every failure returns as signal."

**[1:46]** *(cadence — one word per beat)*
"Observe. Diagnose. Reason. Execute. Learn. Repeat."

**[1:52]**
"An autonomous flywheel. Every experiment makes Aperture smarter."

---

## FINAL SCENE — Reveal · `#final`

> Full-bleed. The iris opens. The wordmark blooms one last time.

**[1:58]**
"Aperture. The AI Data Intelligence Layer."

**[2:03]** *(soft, certain)*
"Understanding how AI learns — not just generating data."

**[2:08]** *(CTA)*
"Build better AI through autonomous data engineering."

> Hold on the mark. Fade to black.
